Bottom line

  • The wallet you shared (0xE147…Fbb4) is the largest GME holder with 30.74B GME. That is 7.3% of supply and the most it has ever held. It has bought on Uniswap since June 2024 and has never sold GME on a DEX.
  • It is not alone. With conservative rules I link 68 more wallets to it. Together they hold 64.0B GME (15.2% of supply), more than the whole Uniswap pool (52.6B).
  • The group's selling went through side wallets and exchange deposits. About $2.7M of GME went to exchanges, roughly $2.0M of it between November 2024 and February 2025, around the top.
  • The pumps came from a thin pool. About $1.7M of net buying took the market cap from $6.8M to $141M in late 2024. Two-thirds of that buying came from new wallets, while the cluster sold into the demand through exchanges.
  • The very first buyers were a bundle. Twenty-seven wallets from two funders took 42% of supply in the first block for 2.5 ETH and sold all of it within weeks. They are gone, and nothing links them to your wallet.
  • Of 8,923 wallets that held at least 10M GME in its first month, 367 still hold any. The few left are still trimming.
  • The market is flat. Over the last 90 days, $707k was bought and $702k sold on DEX. Holder count has slowly declined since November 2025.

My read: GME is a dormant, concentrated memecoin. Its largest owner group is still adding slowly, but no new demand is following it. Buying now is a bet that this group pumps the token again. Size it as money you can lose completely.

This is a reading of public on-chain data, not financial advice. Wallet links are evidence of shared control, not proof of who owns them.

The token

The contract is clean. The risk is who holds it.

GME launched on 13 May 2024 at 03:45 UTC with 420.69B tokens and 9 decimals. It is a standard stealth-launch contract. Ownership is renounced, buy and sell tax is 0%, wallet-to-wallet tax is 0% and the size limits are removed. There is no mint function. The deployer can still swap or withdraw the contract's own leftover launch tax (0.57B GME). It cannot touch anyone else's tokens.

All Uniswap V2 liquidity tokens sit in the burn address, so the liquidity can't be pulled. The pool holds 130.6 WETH and 52.6B GME, about $608k in total. That is thin. A $10,000 buy moves the price about 6.7%, a $50,000 buy about 36%, and selling 1B GME about −4%.

How the price was pumped

A thin pool, held supply, a crowd, and sales off the DEX

GME's Uniswap liquidity is burned, so nobody can add or remove it. The price is the ratio of ETH to GME in the pool, and the two multiplied together stay fixed. So the price rises with the square of the ETH buyers put in. From 10 October to 12 December 2024, about 430 ETH (~$1.7M) of net buying took the market cap from $6.8M to $141M. That is about $80 of market cap for every $1 of net buying. Today, about $126k of net buying would double the price and about $657k would make it 10×.

  1. Own the supply first. Going into October 2024 the cluster held 67.9B GME, about three times the 23.3B in the pool. Most of it was bought when the market cap was $4–15M. Supply held off the market keeps the pool thin.
  2. A catalyst and an audience. The first run started on 3 June 2024, when Roaring Kitty's return sent GameStop stock up. 23.6B GME left the pool that day, and your wallet made its first buy. The second run came with the late-2024 memecoin rally. GME started moving through MEXC's wallets on 5 November 2024, and Gate.io listed it as GAMESTOP on 16 December, after the top. Promotion on X happens off-chain and isn't in this data.
  3. The crowd does most of the buying. In each run about 65% of net buying came from wallets that had never held GME. It was spread over 1,400 to 4,100 buyers, with the top ten at 16–25%. The cluster's own buying was 5–9%. The 53 largest outside buyers were first funded by unrelated wallets or by Coinbase, Binance or Kraken. Nothing shows the cluster funding them.
  4. Sell somewhere the chart doesn't show it. From November 2024 to February 2025, cluster wallets bought $1.75M of GME on Uniswap and sent $2.0M of GME to exchanges. On the Uniswap chart they look like buyers. The selling happens on exchange order books, and arbitrage bots carry that pressure back to the pool. Other holders sold $7.1M net in the final leg and $12.3M after the top.

Not visible on-chain: posts and calls on X or Telegram, paid promoters, market making or wash trading on MEXC, and wallets funded straight from exchanges that couldn't be linked. Part of the new-wallet buying could still be theirs, but it is spread across thousands of wallets.

Exchanges and supply waiting to sell

The pool is the only market, and most underwater holders sit still

CoinGecko lists one market for this contract: the Uniswap V2 pool. MEXC's "GME" pair is the Solana GME, a different token. Gate.io listed this contract as GAMESTOP in December 2024 and has since delisted it. Bybit couldn't be checked from here, and CoinGecko shows no Bybit market for it. Small amounts still move through a MEXC wallet ($33k in and $76k out in October 2026), but MEXC's public market list has no pair for this contract.

Where several venues do list a small token, arbitrage keeps their prices together, so what limits a pump is the combined depth. For tokens this size the order books are usually thinner than the pool. The Solana GME on MEXC has about $100–180 of orders within 2% of its price. GME's Uniswap pool absorbs about $3,000 before it moves 2%.

GME has had eight bounces of 1.5–2.2× since March 2025. In them, 30–62% of the coins sold were sold at a loss. Only 4–14% came from holders selling at less than half their cost, and coins held longer than six months made up 3–16% of the selling. Recent traders sell into bounces. The deeply underwater, long-held supply has mostly stayed put. All eight bounces faded, including the latest: 2.2× to $4.2M in August 2026, then back to $2.3M.

Where the 420.69B GME sit

    The decision

    Does it make sense to buy?

    In favour

    • The contract is clean: ownership renounced, 0% tax, liquidity burned.
    • The market cap is back near its launch level, and holders paid about 10 times today's price on average.
    • 63% of held supply hasn't moved in a year. Little of it is in profit, so few holders have a reason to take profit.
    • The largest holder keeps adding, has never sold on a DEX from its main wallet, and has capital.

    Against

    • One connected group owns more GME than the pool, so its decisions set the price.
    • That group already took money out near the top through side wallets and exchanges, while the main wallet kept holding.
    • It moves coins into fresh wallets, 38.8B in two days in September 2025, which makes it harder to follow.
    • Demand has been flat for six months and holders are slowly declining. For 22 months, each rally has peaked lower than the one before.
    • Liquidity is thin, and no exchange lists this contract today (Gate.io delisted it). Your exit goes through the same pool as theirs, and they hold far more.
    • The same group also runs pepecoin, BOOE and BasedAI positions. GME is one bag among several.

    I wouldn't call this an investment. It is a speculation on what one group does next. If you take a position, keep it to money you can lose entirely, buy in small pieces with tight slippage, and watch for these signals:

    • Exit signals: GME leaving the September 2025 wallets (0xfa07…94f4, 0xd8cb…d493, 0x1df2…6356, 0xcc9a…91e3, 0xff58…9d50). Any cluster wallet depositing to MEXC or the unidentified exchange. Your wallet selling on a DEX for the first time.
    • Signs of real demand: holder count rising for several weeks, DEX buys clearly above sells, the pool's WETH reserve growing above 130 WETH, and large new buyers with no link to the cluster.
